Economic and Financial Analysis

Reviewing cost structure and capital need, analysing projected revenues and identifying key assumptions and risks.

Economic and financial analysis answers a practical question: where does the project stand economically, based on the information available? Cost structure, capital requirements at each stage, projected revenue sources and the sensitivity of the outcome to changed assumptions are all examined.

The analysis relies on information provided by the project owner. It is not a substitute for an audit, a formal valuation or a review by a licensed institution. Its purpose is to clarify assumptions and risks so that decisions about the next steps are better informed.

What this service covers

  • Initial review of the cost structure and capital requirement
  • Analysis of projected revenues and costs based on the information provided
  • Identification of key assumptions and risks
  • Preparing the information needed to decide on next steps

Possible outputs

  • A summary of costs, capital requirement and projected revenue sources
  • A list of key assumptions and material risks
  • Recommendations for additional information to improve accuracy
This analysis is not a substitute for a licensed audit or formal valuation and is based on the information provided.
