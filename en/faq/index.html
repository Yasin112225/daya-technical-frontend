FAQ

Answers to common questions

The answers are deliberately cautious; where an answer depends on a specific project or agreement, that is stated.

What services does Daya Technical provide?

Our services cover initial project assessment, economic and financial analysis, financing structure design, assessment of tokenization of economic interests, project information preparation and coordination of specialist services. Each service begins after agreement on scope, timing and outputs, and none replaces licensed services such as audit or formal valuation.

Which projects can submit a request?

Industrial, capital-intensive projects with a defined financing need: refining and oil and gas, solar power, power generation, mining, manufacturing and industrial infrastructure. The project should have a defined asset or service, and the applicant should be authorised to present its information. Final fit is determined during the initial review.

What information is needed for an initial review?

Basic applicant information, a description of the project and its current stage, location, the size of the financing requirement and the status of key documents such as a feasibility study and permits. Missing documents do not prevent submission, but they affect the depth and duration of the review.

Does submitting a request create any cost or commitment?

Submitting an initial request is free of charge and creates no commitment for either party. The cost and terms of each stage are stated before that stage begins, within a defined agreement.

Does submitting a request guarantee financing?

No. No stage of this process guarantees financing, certain profit, a guaranteed return or investment approval. Our aim is to clarify the needs, constraints and options facing the project so that decisions are better informed.

Are all projects suitable for tokenization?

No. Tokenization of economic interests can only be assessed when there is a clear, measurable economic interest, a definable legal and contractual framework, and the regulatory and technical requirements can be met. For many projects the conclusion is that the instrument is not suitable.

Is the information I submit kept confidential?

Access to requests is limited to those authorised to review them, and information is not automatically shared with external services. However, until a dedicated confidentiality agreement is signed, please avoid sending highly confidential documents or sensitive information. Further detail is in the Privacy Policy.

What happens after I submit a request?

The request is recorded with a tracking reference. Its completeness is then reviewed and, if needed, a list of additional information is shared. After that, the possibility of continuing and — where suitable — a scope proposal are communicated. Timing depends on the quality of information and workload.

Can a project be reviewed at an early stage?

Yes. Projects at the concept or feasibility stage can also submit a request. In that case the output focuses more on identifying required information, key assumptions and next steps, and a detailed financial analysis may not be possible.

Can requests be submitted for projects outside Iran?

The request form can be completed for projects outside Iran and records the country of implementation. Whether an engagement is possible depends on the legal, regulatory and operational requirements of that jurisdiction and is determined during review; no fixed geographic coverage is announced at this stage.

Did not find your answer?

For institutional questions or specialist collaboration, use the contact form. Questions about a specific project are best submitted through the project request form.
