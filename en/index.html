About Daya Technical

A structured approach to industrial project finance

Large industrial projects need a precise understanding of their funding requirement, a rigorous economic case, an appropriate structure and coordination between stakeholders. Daya Technical focuses on exactly this: assessing industrial opportunities and the financing routes available to them.

Our work starts with an initial understanding of the project and continues through its documents, information and execution conditions, producing a clearer picture of the needs, constraints and options ahead.

How We Work

A clear path from first request to an informed decision

  1. Initial request

    The project's basic information is submitted through the request form.

  2. Initial screening

    We assess how complete the information is and whether a review can continue.

  3. Additional information

    Where necessary, we provide a list of the documents and data required.

  4. Eligibility assessment

    We determine whether the project fits our scope and review capacity.

  5. Scope proposal

    We set out the proposed stages, outputs and responsibilities.

  6. Agreement and start

    Once agreed, specialist work begins within the defined scope.

  7. Financing routes

    Potential options and their fit with the project are evaluated.

  8. Findings and next steps

    A clearer picture of needs, constraints and options is provided.

Innovative Financing

Tokenization of economic interests: a potential tool, not a default answer

A financing structure should follow the project's economic flows, its legal and contractual framework and its execution conditions. Tokenization of economic interests can only be explored once those prerequisites have been assessed.

FAQ

Short answers to common questions

What services does Daya Technical provide?

Our services cover initial project assessment, economic and financial analysis, financing structure design, assessment of tokenization of economic interests, project information preparation and coordination of specialist services. Each service begins after agreement on scope, timing and outputs, and none replaces licensed services such as audit or formal valuation.

Which projects can submit a request?

Industrial, capital-intensive projects with a defined financing need: refining and oil and gas, solar power, power generation, mining, manufacturing and industrial infrastructure. The project should have a defined asset or service, and the applicant should be authorised to present its information. Final fit is determined during the initial review.

What information is needed for an initial review?

Basic applicant information, a description of the project and its current stage, location, the size of the financing requirement and the status of key documents such as a feasibility study and permits. Missing documents do not prevent submission, but they affect the depth and duration of the review.

Does submitting a request create any cost or commitment?

Submitting an initial request is free of charge and creates no commitment for either party. The cost and terms of each stage are stated before that stage begins, within a defined agreement.

Does submitting a request guarantee financing?

No. No stage of this process guarantees financing, certain profit, a guaranteed return or investment approval. Our aim is to clarify the needs, constraints and options facing the project so that decisions are better informed.
