About Us

A structured approach to industrial project finance

Daya Technical works on large, asset-based projects: assessing industrial opportunities, analysing economics and finance, designing financing structures and examining where innovative financing instruments may be relevant.

Large industrial projects need a precise view of their funding requirement, a sound economic case, an appropriate structure and coordination between stakeholders to move from concept to execution. Daya Technical focuses on these needs and on the financing routes available to each project.

Our approach starts with an initial understanding of the project, then examines its economic and financial dimensions, capital requirement, documentation and execution conditions so that the next steps can be decided with better information.

Depending on the project, conventional financing routes, contractual structures and — where feasible — innovative approaches such as tokenization of economic interests may also be assessed.

The purpose is a clearer picture of the needs, constraints and options facing each project — not a promise of financing, and not one template applied to every business.

Mission

Helping industrial project owners clarify their funding requirement, understand project economics better and choose a financing path that fits real conditions.

Proposed vision

To become a trusted reference for industrial project financing, built on transparent analysis, respect for legal frameworks and confidentiality of information.

Working principles

Four principles that frame every engagement

Transparency

Scope, responsibilities and limitations are agreed before work begins.

Analytical rigour

Decisions rest on documented information and explicit assumptions.

Confidentiality

Project information is used only for the review and with restricted access.

Fit for purpose

Every project has its own structure; options are assessed on that project's characteristics.

Staged engagement, proportional to available information

Every engagement begins with an initial request and continues once scope, outputs and responsibilities are defined. Incomplete information does not prevent a start, but the depth of analysis depends on the quality of that information.

Working with specialist firms

Some project tasks — legal services, audit, formal valuation, permits or technical assessment — require independent expertise or licensing. Where needed and agreed, Daya Technical coordinates with such firms; that coordination does not mean those services are provided by Daya Technical itself.
