Submit a Project

Request a review and financing assessment

Share your project's basic information so that an initial review and the next steps can be determined. Completing the form does not mean the project is accepted or that financing is guaranteed.

01

Applicant information

Example: +98 21 0000 0000
02

Project information

For example: project owner, representative of the owner, or mandated adviser.
Describe the product or service, capacity, existing infrastructure and the current state of the plan.
03

Financing requirement

An approximate figure; choose the currency in the next field.
04

Additional information

05

Consent and submission

The information you submit is used only to review the request and contact you. Please avoid sending highly confidential or sensitive documents at this stage.
Fields marked with an asterisk are required.

What happens next

  1. Your request is recorded with a tracking reference.
  2. The information is checked for completeness.
  3. If needed, a list of additional information is shared.
  4. The outcome of the initial review is communicated.

Important notes

  • Submitting a request is free of charge.
  • Submitting creates no financing obligation.
  • Access to requests is limited to the authorised review team.
  • Use the contact form for general questions.
