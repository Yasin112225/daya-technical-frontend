Industries

Industrial and Energy Infrastructure

Industrial and energy infrastructure typically has a long payback horizon and a more public character. Its financing structure depends more heavily on tariffs, operating arrangements and risk sharing with public bodies.

Common challenges

  • Long payback periods and sensitivity to discount rates
  • Dependence on tariffs and decisions by public bodies
  • Complexity of operation and maintenance arrangements
  • Construction risk and delays in delivering infrastructure

Information needed for assessment

  • Scope of infrastructure services and target users
  • Expected tariff or fee framework
  • Construction, operation and maintenance cost estimates
  • Status of agreements with public bodies and permits
  • Phasing plan for construction and commissioning

This list is a guide; during review a more precise list is provided for the project.
